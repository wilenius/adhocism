Agentic flooding #LLMagent

+ [A new paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16603) names a development that started early this year: agentic flooding.

+ It basically means that API commons are threatened by AI agents, which spam free-to-use APIs

+ The paper argues that governmental APIs are at risk, but I think the phenomenon is – or will be – much more general.

	+ For example, [Amadeus](https://developers.amadeus.com) discontinued their free API tier for individual developers this year. I suspect this is because too many people had instructed their AI assistants to check flight prices there.

+ See also: [Digital Enclosures]({{< ref "/pages/Digital Enclosures" >}}), even though this more in the Tragedy of the Commons side of things...
