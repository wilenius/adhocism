+ [A new paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01637) that describes an infiltration strategy for AI assistants.

+ Briefly, it injects multiple benign-looking memories that can combine to steer an agent maliciously.

+ For agent-conducted autonomous or semi-autonomous research, at minimum, memory needs source provenance and periodic review at the level of combined claims.
